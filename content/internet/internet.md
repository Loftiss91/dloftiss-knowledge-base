# Internet

Internet is an important part of modern gaming, especially if the gaming is going to be online. In 2026 there are many internet options to choose from, rural areas tend to have few options if any when it comes to internet. When I was younger the area I lived in had one option and it wasn't good at all. 

## Online Gaming

Online gaming requires decent internet to work properly. Latency, packet loss, bandwidth, and connection type all play a crucial role in how well the online gaming experience will be for the user. 
* **Latency** - Measures how fast data travels to the game server and back, under 50ms is an ideal latency for online gaming.
* **Packet Loss** - Fluctuations in ping or dropped data packets cause rubber-banding, lag spikes, and disconnects. 
* **Bandwidth** - The majority of online games only need around 5Mbps when actively playing but users really see benefits of faster speeds when downloading or updating their games.
* **Connection Type** - Ethernet connection is best for online gaming but a Wi-Fi can be manageable, especially in 2026.

## Types of Internet

* **Fiber** - This is the standard for gaming.
* **Cable** - Availability is good, speeds and quality are also fair. Downside is peak hours can see throttles. 
* **Fixed Wireless** - This is going to help those that live in rural areas because of availability but weather plays a major factor in how well it works.
* **Satellite** - An even better upgrade for rural areas.

### Equipment

The equipment needed is pretty straightforward and most Internet Providers are going to offer bundles that include the equipment. Modem, router, and cables are the pieces of equipment needed. This short list has a wide variety of options to meet user needs, purchasing equipment is a great option because you can buy what you want or prefer and save money in the long run instead of equipment leasing from the ISP.
