# Cyberpunk 2077

Cyberpunk 2077 is a single-player open world role-playing game set in the dystopian California metropolis of Night City. You play as V, a cybernetically enhanced mercenary fighting for survival. 

## World Scale and Immersion

Night City is massive and there is so much visually, neon street markets, sketchy back alleys, and huge skyscrapers. Similar to [[star-citizen|Star Citizen]], having zero loading screens is what adds to the immersion for me. I can walk straight out of my apartment, hop in my car, drive across town, and get into a shootout without the game stopping to load once. Every district has its own gangs, vibe, and side jobs that make the city actually feel alive. Cyberpunk 2077 is a visual masterpiece!

## Playstyle Options

- **Solo Tank:** Focus on Body and dermal armor upgrades so you can tank damage during fights.
- **Sandevistan:** Swap out your hacking deck for an operating system that slows down time, this allows you to clear an entire gang in seconds.
- **Netrunner:** Uses quickhacks to overheat enemy weapons, blind guards, or annihilate an entire gang through their own security cameras. 
#### Netrunner Build

Instead of standard fantasy armor, you upgrade your character by ripping out parts of your body and replacing them with military-grade cyberware. You can build V to fit whatever playstyle you want. You want to create a netrunner V? You have complete control on how you build V! A netrunner build is a focus on quickhacks. Here you prioritize the intelligence stat and equip gear that will enhance your cyberdeck.
