# Escape from Tarkov

Escape from Tarkov is a PvEvP hardcore tactical extraction shooter. The game is set a fictional quarantined area of Russia. Escape from Tarkov is in my opinion the original extraction shooter! The gameplay is centered around 'raids', these raids are instanced matches or lobbies. You enter the chosen map, the match has a timer and you must extract before that timer reaches zero. 

## Combat and Realism

The combat and realism is what makes the game intense, every encounter is high-risk. If you die you lose everything on your character. Gunfights are intense, armor & bullet penetration play major roles in these gunfights. The medical and injury system is very detailed and punishing, ignoring an injury can lead to more injuries or even death during a raid. The audio adds to the realism, every footstep or sound can be a clue to what is near you. Footsteps on wood might indicate exactly where that enemy is located!

> Map knowledge is key in survival.


## Extraction

Extraction is fairly simple for a player who knows the maps, for a new player this task becomes a nightmare. My first time playing Escape from Tarkov goes all the way back to early 2017. I had never played anything like it, I was immediately hooked! The first successful extraction was a huge accomplishment, I still had little to no knowledge of the map but I survived the raid! The more I played the more I learned the map and the extraction became easier, it was everything in between that became more intense.
