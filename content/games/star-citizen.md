# Star Citizen

Star Citizen is an MMO (massively multiplayer online game) space simulation sandbox. The universe is persistent, players can travel between three different systems with zero loading screens. You create a character and choose your own career and create your own story. 

## World Scale and Immersion

There are currently three star systems, each with their own planets, moons, space stations, and life. You can fly from one star system to another, fly straight through a planets atmosphere down to the surface and land. You can traverse that planet or moon on foot or ground vehicle and explore. The zero loading screens is what really adds to the immersion of the game. Every item in the game is physicalized. The persistent universe is similar to [[gray-zone-warfare|Gray Zone Warfare]], Star Citizen is a lot larger in size though. When you log back into the game on Star Citizen you will wake up wherever you left off. If you went to sleep and logged out on your ship in deep space, you will wake up in the bed on your ship in deep space. If you logged out at a random space station, you will wake up in a bed of the Hab on that space station. If you die, your body along with all of your gear will remain in the universe until that body is looted. 

## Vehicles

There are hundreds of flyable ships and ground vehicles. There are size classes, these range from small snubs to massive capital ships. Every ship is built for a specific role or gameplay loop, there are medical ships, combat ships, cargo ships, mining ships, and many more. The same goes for the ground vehicles. Each ship is uniquely designed by the developers. 

## Sci-fi

Star Citizen shares a few similarities with one of my all-time favorite games, [[destiny|Destiny]]. The main similarity being the sci-fi worlds, but the co-op multiplayer is what I love about both games. Star Citizen encourages players to team up to take on missions and defeat bosses. In Destiny there are dungeons and raids that require teamwork and co-op gameplay. I have experienced massive ship battles against AI enemies that would be impossible solo, but with a group they are extremely fun and challenging. These encounters remind me of Destiny and how awesome that game was.
