# Gray Zone Warfare

Gray Zone Warfare is a PvEvP open world tactical shooter, it is one of a kind. Not your typical extraction shooter, not a 'battlefield' game, and not an Arma or Squad MilSim. Gray Zone Warfare takes pieces of all these games and makes something different. It has the sandbox element, it has the tactical element, it has that extraction feel, and it also has an open-world that is persistent. It is set in a fictional area of Korea.

## Persistent Open World

The persistent open world is similar in a way to [[star-citizen|Star Citizen]]. There are no 'matches', there are no instant travel or teleportation to objectives, both games rely on real-time travel. They share similarities in high-stakes and risk as well, if you die then you must return to your previous body to retrieve the items you want. Both worlds share the PvEvP as well by mixing AI enemy encounters and the player versus player encounters in a shared space. This adds to that high-stakes and risk, you never know what encounter is coming or when it will happen. 


## Extraction

Extracting from Gray Zone Warfare is fairly simple, return to base camp or enter a friendly combat outpost. The really neat part about Gray Zone Warfare is the open world, there's no timer for your 'raid or match'. You can stay in the field as long as you want or for as little as you want. The typical extraction shooter has limits or boundaries, [[escape-from-tarkov|Escape from Tarkov]] is one of those games. Escape from Tarkov has a raid timer, that timer depends on the map and the world is not completely open. You can travel between maps but doing so ends the current match or instance and a new one begins. 

## Similarities with Escape from Tarkov

Gray Zone Warfare and [[escape-from-tarkov|Escape from Tarkov]] share a few other similarities as well. The hardcore shooters share deep weapon customization, ballistics & armor penetration, intense medical & injury system, inventory UI, and trader missions & reputation. 

#### Weapon Customization

The weapon customization in both of these games is unmatched, the possibilities are endless! Every individual attachment can alter that weapon's stats, forcing you to make decisions based on how you want to play. Need better recoil? You will have to sacrifice that weapons time to aim-down-sights!
