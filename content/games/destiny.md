# Destiny 2

Destiny 2 is by far my favorite multiplayer game. It is a sci-fi looter shooter, you play as a Guardian who is trying to protect Earth's last city. 

## Gunplay

My preferred gameplay in Destiny 2 was the PvP. I have yet to find a game that can do gunplay as well as Destiny 2. The satisfaction that the gunplay gives is unmatched in so many ways. Sliding or dodging in and out of gunfights, mid-air shots landing, and even being caught off guard and still winning the gunfight, nothing beats that adrenaline. 

## Gear

The weapon and armor possibilities in Destiny was incredible, where every perk or stat matters. I spent thousands of hours in Destiny 2, I would say a majority of that time was farming gear that I wanted with specific stats or perks. There were many different gameplay activities that awarded different armor or weapons. The variety the game brought kept the grind from feeling painful, if you got sick of grinding for one item you could swap to a different grind. The grind also never ends! You got the armor you wanted? Cool, there are so many different possibilities you better get to grinding for a different armor set that has different perks.
