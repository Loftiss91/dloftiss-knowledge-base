# Peripherals

Peripherals are the external gear you plug into your PC to actually control the game, see what is happening, and hear audio. Having good peripherals makes a huge difference in comfort and reaction time, especially in competitive or simulation games.

## Core Peripherals
* **Mouse & Keyboard** - The standard input setup for PC gaming; gaming mice offer higher DPI and polling rates, while mechanical keyboards give faster, more reliable key presses.
* **Monitor** - Your display output; refresh rate (144Hz+) and response time matter just as much as resolution for smooth gameplay.
* **Headset / Audio** - Good headphones or a headset let you hear directional audio cues like footsteps and communicate clearly with teammates.
* **Controllers & Flight Sticks** - Great alternatives for racing, flying, or space sims where analog movement beats a keyboard.
